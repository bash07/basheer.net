## About Me

I'm Basheer — a software engineer with a deep passion for **embedded systems**, **artificial intelligence**, and the art of building things that simply work.

With experience spanning firmware development, cloud infrastructure, and AI integration, I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and software — where bits meet atoms.

## About This Blog

This blog is both a creative outlet and a technical experiment. It serves two purposes:

1. **Sharing Knowledge** — Writing about technology, engineering, and AI in a way that's practical and actionable
2. **Building in Public** — Documenting how this fully automated blog platform works, from AI content generation to zero-cost deployment

### The Zero-Cost Stack

Every component of this blog runs on free-tier services:

- **[Hugo](https://gohugo.io)** — Blazing-fast static site generator
- **[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com)** — Free static site hosting
- **[Cloudflare](https://cloudflare.com)** — CDN, DNS, and SSL at no cost
- **[GitHub Actions](https://github.com/features/actions)** — Automated CI/CD pipeline
- **[Google Gemini](https://ai.google.dev)** — AI-powered content assistance
- **[N8N](https://n8n.io)** — Workflow automation on a free GCP VM

### How Content is Created

Posts on this blog are created through a hybrid approach:

1. **AI-Assisted Generation** — Google Gemini generates initial drafts based on curated topic prompts
2. **Human Review** — Every post is reviewed and refined before publication
3. **Automated Publishing** — N8N commits the final markdown to GitHub, triggering an automatic build and deploy
